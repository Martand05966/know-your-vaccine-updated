Common questions

Is this check a medical diagnosis?

No. It is an indicative guide based on commonly followed vaccine schedules for your age and health. Please confirm with a doctor before vaccination, especially if you are pregnant or have a medical condition.

Why do adults need vaccines?

Protection from some childhood vaccines fades over time, some vaccines like flu are needed every year, and risks such as pneumonia and shingles rise with age and chronic illness.

How does at-home vaccination work?

Book the vaccine on PharmEasy. It reaches you in a cold-chain box and a trained professional gives it at home. Vaccine administration is free.

Can I take more than one vaccine on the same day?

Many vaccines can be given together, but it depends on the vaccines and your health. Our doctors can help you plan the schedule.
